# Lead playbook — Harbor Interiors (sample company)

We design and fit kitchens and wardrobes for homes in Pune and Mumbai.

## What a good lead looks like

Score each point, then add them up. Measure "within 3 months" from the
date the enquiry was received, not from today. A lead who gives a month with
no year means its next occurrence.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Says when they need it (within 3 months) | 3 |
| Gives a budget of ₹5 lakh or more | 3 |
| Owns the property or is the decision maker (a family member who will "decide later" does not count) | 2 |
| Asks for a site visit or a call | 2 |
| In Pune or Mumbai | 1 |
| Only asking for a price list, nothing else | -2 |

- 8 or more: HOT
- 4 to 7: WARM
- 3 or less: COLD

## Which drafts need a closer look

A draft is **ROUTINE** when the lead is HOT, in Pune or Mumbai, wants work on
a home, states their own budget and timing, and asks for nothing the offer
below does not cover. A person can approve these in one batch.

A draft is **REVIEW**, and is read on its own before it is approved, when any
of these is true:

- the lead is outside Pune and Mumbai
- the work is not a home (an office, a shop)
- the score depends on a signal the scout marked provisional or asked about
- the lead asks for a price, a discount, a completion date, or anything else the offer does not cover (asking when we can visit is fine: the visit is the offer)

## The offer

A free site visit and a written estimate within three working days.
Nothing else is promised in a first reply: no prices, no discounts, no dates.

## Tone

Warm, short, plain. Use the person's first name. Reply in the language they
wrote in (English, Hindi or Marathi). Sign off as "Meera, Harbor Interiors".
