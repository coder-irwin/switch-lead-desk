# Lead playbook — Harbor Interiors (sample company)

We design and fit kitchens and wardrobes for homes in Pune and Mumbai.

## What a good lead looks like

Score each point, then add them up.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Says when they need it (within 3 months) | 3 |
| Gives a budget of ₹5 lakh or more | 3 |
| Owns the property or is the decision maker | 2 |
| Asks for a site visit or a call | 2 |
| In Pune or Mumbai | 1 |
| Only asking for a price list, nothing else | -2 |

- 8 or more: HOT
- 4 to 7: WARM
- 3 or less: COLD

## The offer

A free site visit and a written estimate within three working days.
Nothing else is promised in a first reply: no prices, no discounts, no dates.

## Tone

Warm, short, plain. Use the person's first name. Reply in the language they
wrote in (English, Hindi or Marathi). Sign off as "Meera, Harbor Interiors".
